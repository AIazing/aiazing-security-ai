Another customer's invoice is accessible

A signed-in customer can read a test invoice belonging to another customer, exposing billing details.

Customer A's session returns Customer B's invoice.

HTTP 200
invoice.owner = customer_b

Demonstrated with test accounts and synthetic records.

  2. Reject cross-account access.
